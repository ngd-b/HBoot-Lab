## English

> A speaking score is useless if it doesn't show what to practice next.
>
> My English app reports each sentence separately. Learners can retry weak lines or move to another scenario in the same category.
>
> The next practice starts with a specific mistake.

> 如果口语评分没有告诉你接下来该练什么，这个分数就没什么用。

>
> 下一次练习从一个具体问题开始。
