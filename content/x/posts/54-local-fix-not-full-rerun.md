# 54｜A small cutout flaw doesn’t need a full rerun

- 日期：2026-09-23
- 状态：已发布
- 字符数：271

## English

> A cutout has one tiny leftover. Do you really need to rerun the whole image?
>
> I added an adjustable erase brush with undo to my image editor. For a small stray mark, fix just that area. If the subject itself is missing detail, undo: erasing can’t bring those pixels back.

## 中文校对

> 抠图只剩一点小瑕疵，真的要整张重新处理吗？
>
> 我给图片编辑器加了可调大小、支持撤销的擦除笔。小块残留就修局部；如果缺的是主体细节，就撤销——擦除没法把那些像素补回来。
