# 45｜One dialogue, two voices

- 日期：2026-09-16
- 状态：已发布

## English

> A two-person dialogue played in one voice sounds like one person talking to themselves.
>
> I gave each role in my English app its own Kokoro voice, so the learner can hear which line is theirs and which is the other speaker's reply.
>
> The conversation is now followable by ear, not just by reading the labels.

## 中文校对

> 双人对话用同一个声音播放，听起来像一个人在自言自语。
>
> 我给英语应用里的两个角色分别配上不同的 Kokoro 音色，学习者能听出哪句是自己要说的、哪句是对方的回答。
>
> 现在不用盯着文字标签，靠耳朵就能跟上对话。
